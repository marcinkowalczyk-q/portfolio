I feel most comfortable at the intersection of UX, product, and technology. I enjoy working on problems where the challenge goes beyond designing screens and requires understanding the system behind them such as processes, rules, data, roles, and dependencies.

I am particularly drawn to ambitious, complex problems, especially in products where design needs to connect user needs, system logic, and real world constraints. I do not see design as just working on screens, but as a way to simplify processes that are difficult to understand, use, or evolve.

I am also interested in applying AI to product design, especially in areas like decision support, personalization, and automation.
